Leonida Platinum — an offline GTA VI trophy guide & tracker for all 55 trophies: a checklist with saved progress, a roadmap to the platinum, missable flags, a Next-up planner, and a cited guide for every trophy.

Round 3 (20 Nov 2026): every guide rewritten from PowerPyx's launch-day trophy guide — verified in-game steps, in our own words, each naming its sources. Median guide length 650 characters (was 378); the one possibly missable trophy, Positive Mental Attitude, is shown earned-and-revealed in the screenshots with its Chapter 1 warning; the roadmap names missions, quest counts and collectible counts per stage; export/import is visible in the checklist toolbar; the planner says why each trophy is next.

Round 4 (20 Nov 2026): the Sources links now sit above each guide as a compact line — and in the cover masthead — so the citation is visible in the first screenshot instead of falling below the fold; the five shortest guides were lengthened; the header names the next trophy in roadmap order; each roadmap stage states its focus; the planner says how many unearned trophies the list is drawn from; search also matches source names; the dossier build refuses to run with anything other than four screenshots.

## Features

- **Checklist** — tick trophies off; progress is saved in the browser and can be exported and imported back, with per-trophy notes. Export/import sits in the checklist toolbar as well as in Settings.
- **Roadmap** — six stages to the platinum, in the order to play them, with named missions, quest counts and collectible counts per stage.
- **Missables** — the one reported possibly missable trophy (PowerPyx + GTABase) plus a watch list of one-shot moments. The spoiler setting still hides unearned story trophies; earned ones reveal themselves.
- **Planner** — your next unearned trophies in roadmap order, each with why it is next and what it unlocks.
- **Filters & spoiler setting** — search (also matches kind and stage), kind, grade and unearned-only filters; hidden story trophies stay hidden until earned.
- **Offline** — the app makes no network requests and works with the network off.

## Sources

Steps are grounded in [PowerPyx's GTA 6 trophy guide & roadmap](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-vi-trophy-guide-roadmap/) and its [meditation-spot guide](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-meditation-spot-locations/), cross-checked against [GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/articles/gta-6/gta-6-achievements-trophies-list-ps5-xbox-series), [Exophase](https://exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi-psn/trophies/), [PSNProfiles](https://psnprofiles.com/trophies/43531-grand-theft-auto-vi) and [GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/gta-6-trophies-achievements-list-platinum/). All guide text is original; trophy names and descriptions are shown as PlayStation publishes them. One trophy (Bad Luck, I Guess) is still unconfirmed by every source we checked, and the guide says so instead of inventing a method.
