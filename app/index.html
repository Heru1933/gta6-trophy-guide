      Missables & the watch list

      One trophy is reported as possibly missable. The rest below are one-shot moments worth doing carefully — none is confirmed missable, and every one can be retried in free roam as far as the official list shows.

      Why only one confirmed missable?

      GTABase and PowerPyx both flag Positive Mental Attitude as the only trophy that might be missable, tied to a meditation session with Lucia in Chapter 1 — though neither confirms it is required. No other trophy on the official list has been flagged as missable by any source we checked. Story missions can be replayed, and the robbery and stunt trophies can be retried in free roam.
